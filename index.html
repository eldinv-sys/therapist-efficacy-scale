About this questionnaire

Rapport is an important part of the therapeutic relationship. Drawing on person-centered ideas associated with Carl Rogers, these statements focus on how comfortable, accepted, heard, and respected a client feels with their therapist.

Instructions: Think about your experience with your therapist throughout treatment. Select one response for each statement, from 1 (Strongly disagree) to 5 (Strongly agree).

Privacy: This is a classroom demonstration. Your answers stay in this browser page and are not sent to a server or saved to local storage. Closing or refreshing the page clears them.
